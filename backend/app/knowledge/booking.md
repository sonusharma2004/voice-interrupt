# Table bookings

We take reservations for the communal nook and the two window two-tops. Walk-ins always welcome for counter seats.

## Rules

- Party size: 2 to 6 people. Solo guests sit at the bar — no booking needed.
- Hours we book: 8:00am to 2:00pm, on the hour and half-hour.
- Same-day bookings are fine if the slot is open.
- 15-minute grace. After that the table is released.
- We hold a table for 75 minutes. Not a dinner service — we are a café.
- Name and party size required. Phone optional.
- No booking fee. Cancel by voice or a note on the door if you are a no-show twice we may refuse the next hold.

## Capacity

Each half-hour slot can hold two bookings, totaling at most 8 guests.
We do not book the entire room for private events during service.

## What Mira should do

Check availability before promising. Repeat the time, party size, and name back once before confirming. If a slot is full, offer the nearest open time the same day.
