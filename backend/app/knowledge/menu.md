# Harbor & Rye Menu

Neighborhood café at 418 Willow Street. Warm, slightly briny, rye-forward.

## Coffee & drinks

- House drip — 3.50. Rotating single origin. Oat or almond +0.75, dairy free of charge.
- Cortado — 4.50. Equal espresso and steamed milk. Best with whole or oat.
- Flat white — 5.00. Double ristretto, microfoam. Vegan with oat milk.
- Cappuccino — 4.75.
- Pour-over — 6.00. 6 minute brew. Ask for today's bean.
- Chai latte — 5.00. House-spiced, not too sweet.
- Hot chocolate — 4.25. Dark Valrhona. Oat works well.
- Sparkling lemon tonic — 3.75. No caffeine.
- Cold brew — 4.75. Steeped 16 hours.

Milk: whole, 2%, oat, almond. Default for "regular" is whole.

## Kitchen

- Avocado rye — 9.50. Smashed avocado, chili, lemon, herbs on toasted rye. Vegan.
- Smoked salmon tartine — 13.00. Dill yogurt, capers, red onion, rye.
- Egg & cheddar biscuit — 8.50. Soft scramble, sharp cheddar, herb biscuit. Vegetarian.
- Mushroom toast — 11.00. Roasted oyster mushrooms, garlic butter, thyme. Vegetarian. Can be made vegan (olive oil instead of butter).
- Seasonal soup — 7.00. Changes daily. Cup only after 11.

## Pastry

- Butter croissant — 4.25. Laminated in-house. Not vegan.
- Almond croissant — 5.50. Frangipane.
- Kouign-amann — 5.00. Caramelized sugar, very rich.
- Coconut chia cup — 5.50. Coconut yogurt, chia, berries, rye crumble. Vegan and gluten-free if you skip the crumble.
- Morning bun — 4.50. Orange-cardamom.

## Dietary notes

Vegan as written: avocado rye, coconut chia cup (no crumble), oat flat white / cortado / drip, lemon tonic, cold brew (black).
Mushroom toast is vegan if we swap butter for olive oil — say so when ordering.
Gluten-free: coconut chia cup without crumble, drinks, soup (confirm the day).
Nuts: almond croissant, almond milk. Kitchen is not a nut-free room.

## Ordering style

Items can take mods: extra shot, oat, no foam, warmed croissant, butter on the side.
We do not do blended drinks or syrups beyond a single vanilla or honey request.
