# Hours & house

Harbor & Rye — 418 Willow Street, ground floor, green awning.

## Hours

- Tuesday–Friday: 7:30am – 3:00pm
- Saturday–Sunday: 8:00am – 3:00pm
- Monday: closed
- Last coffee order 2:40pm. Kitchen closes 2:30pm.
- Holiday hours posted on the door; assume closed Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

## Getting here

Two blocks south of the Willow streetcar stop. Bike racks on the side alley. Street parking is metered until 6pm weekdays. No dedicated lot.

## Atmosphere

Counter service. About 28 seats, including a communal rye-colored table. Laptops are fine on weekdays before 11; weekends we ask for one seat per person if we are full. Dogs on the sidewalk bench only.
